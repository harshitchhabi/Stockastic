Privacy

This site runs a live market simulation event. Nothing here involves real money or real trading.

What we keep. The name and email you register with (or that Google shares when you choose "Continue with Google"), your trades and portfolio in the simulation, and the times you were connected. If you sign in with Google we receive only your name and verified email address.

Why. To run the event, decide the results and prizes, and resolve any dispute.

Who sees it. The event organisers. We do not sell or share it with anyone else. Other participants never see your email or your holdings.

How long. Until the organisers delete the event data after the results are settled. Ask an organiser to delete your account earlier if you wish.

Cookies. None are used for tracking. Signing in with Google uses one short-lived cookie that only carries a random value for that sign-in.
